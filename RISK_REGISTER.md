### Top two confirmed risks for corrective action
1. **A05-R02 (score 6):** the highest confirmed score. Without enforcement, every other rule in AGENTS.md depends on honest self-reporting by the agent.
2. **A05-R01 (score 4):** a wrong reason is visible to students, which breaks the feature contract ("agrees with the deterministic result"). The fix is small and testable.

A05-R03 also scores 4, but its fix needs major version upgrades (vitest 4, vite 6+). That is not a minimal mitigation and AGENTS.md requires a reviewed plan for it, so it stays open as a release condition.
